# V1-16 Editable session history and permanent group deletion

Completed locally on October 6, 2026.

## Behavior and decisions

- Owners and managers can save chosen participants directly to History without a restaurant or delivery address. Logged sessions keep their actual creation date and no completion timestamp until finished.
- Managers can manually finish their sessions without a restaurant. Current group owners can edit captured session dates, status, manager, restaurant, address, participants, names, food responses, items, quantities, prices, and notes, including finished sessions. Existing item source, variant, and modifier snapshots are retained for retained lines. Server validation calculates totals and rejects foreign saved lines or cross-group edits.
- Editing an existing voting or food-selection workflow converts it to a manual History session; the editor displays this notice. Optional full restaurant setup, voting, and food selection remain available.
- Owners can permanently delete a session after an explicit warning. Admins can permanently delete active or archived groups after an explicit warning covering all sessions/history. Deletion is transactional and removes dependent order, membership, invitation, request, notification, job, and receipt-association records. Accounts, account favorites, catalog, retained audit evidence, and unattached stored file assets remain.
- Archived memberships are excluded from active identity and user profile summaries. Archived groups remain available in admin Groups for permanent deletion.
- Food completion goes directly to finished. Legacy handoff records can still be finished. The redundant View participants action is removed.
- Owner and manager permissions are enforced server-side; ordinary members retain their own participation/food visibility. A platform admin flag alone does not grant session editing rights.
